# How to access RC dashboards?

In order to access the PBI dashboards, we need to go though the training and request access to our dashboards through UAM. This is something we need to follow as a part RLS check.

Please ensure that all complete the mandatory training in HP Employees SABA.

CW employees need to finish the training on Brainshark Or

Amplify Data Collaboration training recording 6.12.2021 (brainshark.com)

1. Login to UAM
2. Click on Request New Access > I am requesting access for myself
3. Search for Profile: Instant Ink Power BI Reports – PRO
4. Click on Request Access
5. Select the Country/ies or Market/s you need to get access to or select WW if all applies.
6. Enter a Business Justification
7. Click on Submit Request

Once you complete the above steps your requests will be sent to Manager to approve on the same. Post approval from manager, Vinod from IIBI team will provide access to our dashboards by creating group under QA dashboard. This process takes 1 to 2 days post your approval.

NOTE: Every 6 months you need to renew the access. You will get automated email with the link to renew your access. Please follow the process from the email to renew your access to any dashboards.
